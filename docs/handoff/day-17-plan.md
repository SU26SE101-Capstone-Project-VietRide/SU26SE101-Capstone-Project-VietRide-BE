## Objective
Deliver passenger-initiated booking cancellation with a policy-driven refund, plus per-operator BookingStats reporting counters. POST /v1/bookings/{id}/cancel validates the trip is still cancellable, computes the refund from the owning operator cancellationPolicy, transitions the booking to CANCELLED, releases held/booked seats on Trip, and emits `booking.booking.cancelled` to the outbox carrying the computed refundAmount. **Refund delivery is EVENT-DRIVEN (Q4 resolved):** Payment consumes `booking.booking.cancelled`, credits the passenger wallet (reusing the Day-16 wallet-refund logic) and emits `payment.wallet.credited`; the existing Booking WalletCreditedIntegrationEventHandler then transitions CANCELLED -> REFUNDED. The cancel response returns the computed refundAmount as a preview (status CANCELLED, refund pending) — cancellation availability no longer depends on Payment being up. BookingStats is an UPSERT counter table maintained by Booking consuming its own lifecycle events, read back via operator + admin endpoints. Closes the Sprint-3 search/book/pay/cancel loop and unblocks the Day-20 demo.
